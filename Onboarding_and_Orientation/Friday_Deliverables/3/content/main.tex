\subsection{Difference between Skills and Competences }
The difference between skills and competences is that a skill is the ability to perform a particular
task, whereas competence is the broader ability to apply skills, knowledge, and judgement effectively
in a given situation.\\

\noindent \textbf{Skills:} A is an ability that you develop through learning, practice, or experience.
It usually relates to performing a specific task.\\

\noindent \textbf{Competence:} A competence is the ability to use your \textbf{knowledge} and \textbf{skills} appropriately to handle a task, solve a problem, or fulfil a responsibility. It can also involve
attitudes, communication and judgement.\\

\noindent\textbf{Normative, Strategic and Systems thinking} are the three major competences required for the sustainability Engineer. \\
