
# Exercise
## Overview
- SDG 17 is about strengthening and revitalising  global partnerships among governments, the private 
sector and civil soceity.

## Core Pillars
- **Finance:** Mobilizing domestic and international resources, increasing foreign direct investment 
for developing nations, and ensuring debt sustainability.

- **Technology:** The promotion of international cooperation science and innovation and the 
transfer of environmentally friendly technologies.

- **Capacity Building:** Strengthening national capabilities for implementing effective, data-driven 
sustainable policies through localized training and knowledge sharing.

## Relevance to sustainability Engineering
SDG 17 provides the collaborative blueprint required to move innovations from the lab bench to 
global implementation:

- **Green Technology Transfer**: Engineers rely on cross-border partnerships to scale renewable energy
grids, waste-to-energy systems, and efficient water infrastructure in regions that need them most.

- **Interdisciplinary Integration**:Complex sustainability challenges require engineers to collaborate closely with data scientists, environmental economists, and municipal policymakers to ensure 
technical designs align with socio-economic realities.
