\section{VFX}
It refers to the \textbf{tool set} and  \textbf{workflow} used to seamlessly integrate
\textbf{Computer Generated Elements (CG)},  \textbf{3D animations} and digital assets into real
world, live action video.

\subsection{Blender's VFX}
Blenders vfx centers around several core features.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Motion tracking and (Camera Object tracking):}
        Blender is capable of analysing both objects and camera motion through live-video-action
        and replicate these movements in 3D space so that virtual 3D objects appear to naturally
        stick and move with the live-action-video.

  \item  \textbf{Masking (Rotoscoping)}
        The ability to create animated shapes (masks) to isolate specific parts of the video.

  \item \textbf{Compositing:}
        A powerful node-based workspace where you layer the live action footage, CG renders,
        special effects, lighting and color grading together to produce the final polished shot.
        Traditional editing software forces into strict top-down bottom stack. Node based
        systems give you total structural freedom.
\end{itemize}
