Today I had to opportunity to learn about Business and Entrepreneurship. Discovered a lot about
business mainly through this exercise session on Building a Business model. We developed a
business model for  \textbf{High yield Fabric Textile manufacturing}. The exercise was aimed
digging into the various Models to take into consideration while building a Business model
namely:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Market Model
  \item Supply chain Model
  \item Value Creation Model
  \item Revenue Model
  \item Distribution Model
  \item Finance Model
\end{itemize}

These sub model help properly define the business model. Kind of like completing a puzzle.

We also had a look into  \textbf{Profit Maximization}. The relationship between revenue and
cost, both of which depend on the level of output.

\begin{center}
  Profit = Total Revenue - Total Cost
\end{center}

We had a look into  \textbf{Marginal Revenue and Marginal Cost}. Marginal revenue is the
addition to total revenue from producing one more unit of output. Whereas Marginal cost is the
addition to total cost from producing one more unit of output. Profit is maximized at the
output where $MR = MC$.\\

We talked about \textbf{Market Share and  Pricing}. My take away on that was gaining market
share is closely tied to offering cheaper prices and that scarcity pushes prices higher.
Another thing I learned is that  \textbf{Natural Monopolies are rare}. There is almost always
some competition or substitute.
