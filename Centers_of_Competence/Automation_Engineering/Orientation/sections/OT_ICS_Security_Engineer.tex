\section{OT/ICS Security Engineer}
OT stands for  \textbf{Operation Technology} and is the monitoring and control of
\textbf{devices},  \textbf{processes}, and  \textbf{infrastructure} through the use of hardware
and software. The ICS part stands for   \textbf{Industrial Control Systems} which is an
overarching term for control systems associated with instrumentation used in industrial sectors
including  the \textbf{SCADA(Supervisory Control And Data Acquisition) system}, distribution
systems, and \textbf{PLC(Programmable Logic Controller)}. The Field of  \textbf{OT/ICS Security
  Engineering} is a fast growing and scarce field in the German automation market with a demand
growth of roughly \textbf{41\%} each year. Median salary is around \euro 85000 with senior roles
capable of commanding higher.
